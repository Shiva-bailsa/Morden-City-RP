05 / COMMUNITY CODE

SERVER RULES

Follow the rules, respect other players and keep roleplay immersive.

§ 1.1WARNING / DEMORGAN

Roleplay Immersion

Keep actions in-character and do not use out-of-character information to gain an in-character advantage.

§ 1.2DEMORGAN

Deathmatching

Attacking another player without a valid roleplay reason is prohibited.

§ 2.1VOID / DEMORGAN

Robbery & Heist Protocol

Criminal scenarios must have reasonable roleplay escalation and fair participation.

§ 3.1DEMORGAN

Green Zones

Designated safe areas are protected from combat and criminal activity.

§ 4.1VOICE MUTE

Voice Etiquette

No microphone spam, disruptive soundboards or intentional voice abuse.

§ 4.2PERMANENT BAN

Toxicity & Harassment

Hate speech, serious harassment and real-life threats are not tolerated.
