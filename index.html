GAME RULES

Keep the city fun and fair for everyone.

01. No cheating, hacking or third-party advantages.
02. No exploiting bugs, glitches or map issues.
03. Respect all players, admins and staff.
04. No harassment, hate speech or toxic behaviour.
05. Follow all roleplay and faction rules.
06. No metagaming or using OOC info in RP.
07. No powergaming or forcing unrealistic actions.
08. No random deathmatch (RDM) or vehicle deathmatch (VDM).
09. No combat logging or leaving to avoid RP consequences.
10. Do not impersonate staff or abuse reports.
11. No scams involving real money, accounts or external trades.
12. Do not spam chat, voice, ads or malicious links.
13. Use realistic names and maintain character behaviour.
14. Do not abuse safe zones or protected locations.
15. Follow staff instructions during active situations.
16. Keep Discord and in-game behaviour within community rules.
17. No revenge killing without a valid roleplay reason.
18. Do not disrupt events, interviews or official faction scenes.
